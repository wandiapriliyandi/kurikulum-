Modul Ajar — Membaca–Memirsa

Tujuan Pembelajaran

  • TP-1: Peserta didik mampu memahami esai argumentatif level B2.
  • TP-6: Peserta didik mampu menunjukkan penguasaan terpadu materi semester dalam latihan dan ujian membaca komprehensif.
  • Indikator: Memahami teks baru setara B2; menjawab pertanyaan rinci, inferensi, dan evaluasi.

Materi Pokok

Materi berfokus pada instrumen dan tata kelola Ujian Membaca Komprehensif Semester 1 (*Summative Reading Assessment*) menggunakan teks esai argumentatif baru (*unseen text*) level CEFR B2 bertema transisi energi di pesantren:

  1. Karakteristik Teks Baru B2: Esai argumentatif terstruktur dengan tesis eksplisit, penalaran berbasis bukti, bantahan kontra-argumen (*rebuttal*), dan simpulan logis berbobot akademis.
  2. Domain Soal Ujian:
  • *Pertanyaan Rinci (Detail)*: Menemukan fakta tersurat secara presisi via teknik *scanning*.
  • *Pertanyaan Inferensi (Inference)*: Menarik simpulan tersirat berbekal petunjuk konteks (*context clues*).
  • *Pertanyaan Evaluasi (Evaluation)*: Menilai validitas argumen dan kelayakan solusi praktis.
  1. Tata Tertib & Penskoran: Kepatuhan protokol mandiri tanpa kamus/gawai, durasi murni 40 menit, dan penskoran objektif.

Kegiatan Pembelajaran

Kegiatan dominan berbahasa Inggris dengan penguatan adab *thalabul ilmi* dan kejujuran (*ash-shidq*) khas pesantren.

Pendahuluan (±10 menit)

  1. Pembukaan & Doa (3 menit): Guru menyapa santri dalam Bahasa Inggris (*"Good morning. Welcome to our reading exam"*), lalu memimpin doa pembuka belajar.
  2. Apersepsi & Motivasi (3 menit): Guru mengingatkan nilai integritas: *"Today's exam measures your B2 reading skills. Remember our pesantren values: honesty (ash-shidq) and responsibility (amanah). Trust your preparation and work independently."*
  3. Tata Tertib & Distribusi (4 menit): Guru membacakan tata tertib (tanpa kamus/gawai, durasi 40 menit) dan membagikan lembar naskah soal serta lembar jawaban tertutup (*face down*).

Inti (±40 menit) — Pelaksanaan Ujian Mandiri

  1. Pemeriksaan Berkas & Identitas (Menit 0–2): Guru memberi instruksi pemeriksaan kelengkapan naskah dan pengisian identitas santri.
  2. Pengerjaan Ujian Mandiri (Menit 2–30):
  • Guru mengumumkan: *"The 40-minute exam begins now. Read attentively and maintain silence."*
  • Santri membaca esai argumentatif B2 secara hening, menerapkan strategi *skimming*, *scanning*, dan analisis bukti tekstual untuk butir inferensi dan evaluasi.
  • Guru menjalankan pengawasan tertib (*proctoring*) untuk menjamin ketenangan dan iklim ujian yang jujur.
  1. Peringatan Waktu (Menit 30–38): Guru mengingatkan sisa waktu 10 menit dan 2 menit terakhir agar santri memfinalisasi seluruh jawaban.
  2. Penghentian & Pengumpulan Berkas (Menit 38–40):
  • Guru menginstruksikan: *"Time is up. Pens down immediately."* Santri meletakkan alat tulis.
  • Berkas soal dan jawaban dikumpulkan tertib per baris, lalu diverifikasi kelengkapannya.

Penutup (±10 menit)

  1. Debriefing (4 menit): Guru mengapresiasi kedisiplinan dan kejujuran santri: *"Alhamdulillah, thank you for your focus and integrity today."*
  2. Tindak Lanjut (3 menit): Guru mengumumkan jadwal evaluasi dan agenda bedah soal (*error analysis*) pada pertemuan ke-18 (*muraja'ah*).
  3. Doa & Salam (3 menit): Guru memimpin doa kafaratul majelis dan menutup kelas dengan salam.

Asesmen

Teks Bacaan Ujian (Unseen Argumentative Essay — Level B2)

Sustainable Energy Transition in Modern Pesantren Communities

In the contemporary discourse on climate responsibility, Islamic boarding schools (*pesantren*) hold strategic potential to drive green transitions. Historically rooted in simplicity (*zuhd*) and communal self-reliance, expanding campus populations now generate rising energy demands. While critics argue that initial capital costs make clean energy impractical for charitable institutions, adopting solar and bioenergy systems directly reflects the Islamic imperative of earthly stewardship (*khalifah fil ardh*).

Financially, distributed renewable energy builds institutional resilience. Rooftop solar panels and biogas digesters substantially lower recurring utility bills. Savings can be redirected toward student scholarships and classical library preservation. Moreover, pilot projects show that organic waste recycling produces organic fertilizer for surrounding rural farmers, reinforcing communal solidarity.

Conversely, conservative administrators express valid concerns regarding technical maintenance. High-efficiency solar inverters and battery banks deteriorate quickly in humid tropical weather without certified technicians, who are rare in rural locations. However, this obstacle should not justify inertia. The sustainable remedy lies in curriculum reform: integrating clean energy maintenance into vocational training for senior santri. By pairing jurisprudential ethics with engineering skills, pesantren become self-sustaining centers of ecological innovation.

Instrumen Soal Ujian

  1. Detail: According to paragraph 2, how does renewable energy directly benefit the educational mission of the pesantren?
  • A. By replacing religious classes with technical workshops.
  • B. By redirecting saved utility expenses into scholarships and library preservation.
  • C. By selling electricity to the national grid.
  • D. By removing manual student duties in farming.
  1. Detail: What primary operational challenge is raised by conservative school administrators?
  • A. Religious opposition against modern technology.
  • B. Rapid equipment degradation in humid weather coupled with a shortage of rural technicians.
  • C. Lack of physical roof space to install solar panels.
  • D. Santri refusal to participate in waste sorting routines.
  1. Inference: What can be inferred from the author's reference to *khalifah fil ardh*?
  • A. Environmental conservation is an inherent spiritual duty, not merely an external secular trend.
  • B. Technological advancement takes precedence over spiritual cultivation.
  • C. Pesantren should isolate themselves completely from outside society.
  • D. Religious education should be replaced by ecological sciences.
  1. Inference: In the final paragraph, the shortage of technical personnel is viewed as:
  • A. An insurmountable barrier that justifies abandoning clean energy projects.
  • B. An opportunity to incorporate renewable technology maintenance into the student curriculum.
  • C. Concrete proof that modern technology undermines spiritual dedication.
  • D. A reason to postpone sustainable initiatives until subsidies arrive.
  1. Evaluation: How effectively does the author resolve the tension between technical costs and ecological transition?
  • A. Weakly, by dismissing operational concerns as unfounded complaints.
  • B. Effectively, by acknowledging maintenance hurdles and resolving them through vocational empowerment.
  • C. Incompletely, by demanding that pesantren depend solely on foreign grants.
  • D. Irrelevantly, by advising administrators to abandon solar projects whenever maintenance costs rise.

Kriteria Ketuntasan dan Pedoman Penskoran

  • Pedoman Penskoran: Tiap butir bernilai 20 poin (Skor maksimal = 100 poin).
  • Kriteria Ketuntasan Minimal (KKM): Nilai KKM ditetapkan 80 poin (minimal 4 butir dijawab benar).
  • Tindak Lanjut:
  • Remedial: Pembimbingan strategi membaca teks argumentatif pada pertemuan ke-18 bagi santri di bawah KKM.
  • Pengayaan: Pemberian bahan esai komparatif kritis bagi santri dengan nilai 100.

Media dan Sumber Belajar

  1. Media: Lembar naskah soal cetak, lembar jawaban santri, stopwatch/jam dinding kelas, kartu tata tertib ujian.
  2. Sumber Belajar: Teks adaptasi *Sustainable Energy Transition in Modern Pesantren*, panduan penilaian membaca standar CEFR Level B2, buku teks Bahasa Inggris kelas 12.

Catatan Refleksi Guru

  • Ketercapaian Pelaksanaan Ujian: Evaluasi ketertiban kelas, kepatuhan santri terhadap tata tertib, dan efektivitas alokasi waktu 40 menit.
  • Pemetaan Kognitif Santri: Mengidentifikasi apakah santri mengalami kendala lebih dominan pada butir inferensi atau evaluasi argumen.
  • Rencana Tindak Lanjut Pertemuan Berikutnya: Mengompilasi butir soal yang paling banyak dijawab keliru untuk dibedah pada sesi *error analysis* pertemuan ke-18.
